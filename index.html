The details, before you connect.

What is Orbit?+

Orbit is companion software for Among Us that adds role visibility, role tags above players, adjustable player tracers, and a player radar.

What are the pricing options?+

Orbit access costs $3 per 14 days, or $20 for lifetime access.

How do I buy access?+

Purchases are not available on this site yet. Once checkout is available, use Buy access to review pricing and purchase terms.

How do I connect it to Among Us?+

Open Orbit, launch Among Us, then press Connect in the Orbit menu. That’s the setup flow.

Can I adjust the tracers?+

Yes. Orbit includes an adjustable tracer radius so you can choose the area around you. Try the radius slider in the interactive preview above.

Is the preview the actual software?+

The website preview is an interactive illustration of the features. It does not connect to Among Us, and the final software interface may look different.

Is Orbit made by the Among Us developers?+

No. Orbit is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Innersloth. Among Us belongs to its respective owners.
